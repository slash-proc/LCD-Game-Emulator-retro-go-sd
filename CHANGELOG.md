### Install

**Core**

- Copy `LCD-Game-Emulator.bin` to `/cores/` on the SD card.
- Place ROMs under `/roms/gw/` (extension `.gw`).
- Requires firmware whose ABI matches `SDK_VERSION` in this repository.
